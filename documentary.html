🎬 Nisargavedh Documentary

"A Decade of perseverance- An Arcade of Trees"

Watch Full Video

A decade of relentless dedication, countless saplings nurtured and a barren land transformed into a thriving ecosystem. This is the story of Nisargavedh. Our documentary captures the incredible journey of turning dreams into reality, where every tree planted became a testament to hope and perseverance.

From the first shovel breaking ground to witnessing wildlife return to their natural habitat, this film chronicles the passion of volunteers, the wisdom of local communities and the unwavering commitment of CHIP Nagpur. Watch how a decade of hard work created an arcade of life, a sanctuary where nature flourishes and future generations find inspiration.

This documentary is a tribute to every hand that planted a seed, every heart that believed in change, and every soul that stood by nature when it needed us the most. It's not just a film, it's a movement captured in frames.
