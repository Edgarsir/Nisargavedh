What We Do

Ecosystem Restoration

We identify degraded land, protect, afforest, conserve water and restore such lands into thriving eco-systems. In the process we promote a sense of ownership in every individual towards the conservation of the planet earth.

Education Beyond Walls

We engage young minds with basics of life skills by helping to understand the natural world and broaden the horizon of our thoughts when we act. Nature for Future.

Sustainable Livelihood

We create opportunities through our project to communities, enhancing the capabilities and assets for both now and the future without deteriorating the environment or extracting the natural resources.

Our Vision

At CHIP Nagpur, our vision is to create a remarkable sustainable eco-restoration initiative that transcends boundaries of time, revitalizes ecosystems under deprivation of the past and inspires communities for the future. We aim to forge a harmonious relationship between nature and humanity and create green spaces for the future.

By engaging local communities, fostering partnerships, advocating for sustainable practices, and empowering the next generation, we strive to create a greener, healthier and more resilient environment for children and young adults, ensuring a brighter future for all.

Our Mission

  • Enabling the restoration of the ecosystem and establishing it as a vital corridor for wildlife movement between Bor Tiger Reserve and Umred Karhandla Wildlife Sanctuary.
  • Fostering an immersive open-air learning environment, embracing students of all ages and generations, thereby encouraging a closer relationship with Mother Nature.
  • Providing local people with access to fundamental healthcare resources while equipping them with the knowledge they need to live sustainably.
  • Empowering women holistically by facilitating economic freedom, promoting their mental health and encouraging cleanliness practices.

Our Goal

At CHIP Nagpur, we aim to educate 500+ rural children over the next decade. In partnership with the Maharashtra Forest Department, having already planted 46,500 trees and restored 60 acres, we're committed to revitalizing another 100 acres through 100,000+ tree saplings and soil moisture conservation. We strive to engage 50,000+ citizens with nature, support 200 local families in sustainable livelihoods, and back 10 research projects.

Policy Alignment: SDGs and NEP 2020

Alignment with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):

  • SDG 4 – Quality Education: Environmental education and nature-based learning
  • SDG 6 – Clean Water and Sanitation: Groundwater recharge and watershed health
  • SDG 13 – Climate Action: Carbon sequestration and ecosystem restoration
  • SDG 15 – Life on Land: Native forest restoration and biodiversity enhancement
  • SDG 17 – Partnerships for the Goals: Collaboration with corporates and communities

Alignment with National Education Policy (NEP) 2020:

  • Experiential and outdoor learning environments
  • Environmental awareness and sustainability education
  • Hands-on learning beyond classrooms
  • Ecological understanding with social responsibility
  • Holistic development through nature exposure
