Active Projects

Protection

To aid the protection of planted saplings from cattle and man-made threats, this project fulfils an important line of defense and demarcation of the project area.

Irrigation

In order to ensure a regulated and consistent irrigation, supply of water through drip irrigation is set up throughout the forest to provide adequate water for plants to survive and thrive.

Plantation

Carefully nurtured and prepared saplings are planted in the pits prepared via established methods undertaken to ensure the survival.

Soil Moisture Conservation Works (SMC)

SMC works like loose boulder structures, gabion structures, van talav, water trenches, etc. are made to ensure water conservation and retainment of moisture in soil.

Maintenance and Care

Post plantation, regular monitoring, caring and replacement, if necessary are made to ensure the plants' sustained health and survival.

Key Focus Areas

  • Ecosystem restoration and native species plantation
  • Post-plantation care and monitoring
  • Drip irrigation and water management
  • Land protection with wire fencing
  • Community involvement in forest maintenance
  • Biodiversity conservation and habitat creation
